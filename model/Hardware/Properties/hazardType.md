# hazardType

## Summary

Hazards are potential sources of harm, danger, or adverse effects to people, property, the environment, or systems.

## Description

Hazards are potential sources of harm, danger, or adverse effects to people, property, the environment, or systems. They can arise from various sources and contexts, and understanding them is key to preventing accidents, injuries, or damage. A hazard is defined by the standard in the StandardDefineHazard class.
