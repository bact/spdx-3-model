# measurementMethod

## Summary

How the measurement was obtained.

## Description

An explanation of the method used to obtain or estimate the measurement
(e.g., specific instruments, estimation approaches, or tools).

Measurements may be derived from proxies.
For example, the cumulative training compute, measured in FLOPs
(Floating Point Operations), can serve as an estimate for the total
energy consumption of the AI model's development phase.
