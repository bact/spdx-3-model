# DataGenerationMethod

## Summary

Categories of how dataset content was generated.

## Description

Describes whether the content of a dataset was produced by humans, by a
machine, or both.

## Metadata

- name: DataGenerationMethod

## Entries

- humanGenerated: Content was created or collected by humans, or recorded from the real world, without machine generation.
- machineGenerated: Content was generated by a machine, such as an AI model or a simulation (synthetic data).
- mixed: Content combines human-generated and machine-generated parts.
- noAssertion: No assertion is made about how the content was generated.
